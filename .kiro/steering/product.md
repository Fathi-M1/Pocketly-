# Product Steering — Pocketly

## Vision
Make productivity feel rewarding by pairing every task with a living, breathing companion that reflects your consistency.

## Design Principles
- **Delight first** — animations, confetti, and mood shifts must feel earned, not cheap
- **Zero friction capture** — adding a task should take under 5 seconds
- **Companion is the heart** — Pocky's health should feel like a real consequence of the user's habits

## Monetization Strategy (Future)
Premium tier via **RevenueCat** subscriptions:
- Pocky cosmetic skins and accessories
- Advanced analytics (productivity trends, focus scores)
- Cloud sync across devices

## Non-Goals
- Not a project management tool (no subtasks, dependencies, or team features in v1)
- Not a calendar replacement — it supplements, not replaces
